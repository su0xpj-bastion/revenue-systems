# Pricing brief: Insights Platform GA (from Pricing, Jan 2027)

We're launching Insights on March 1, 2027. Three ways to buy:

1. **Team**: $30 per seat per month, minimum 5 seats, sold on 12-month terms and billed annually up front.
2. **API pay-as-you-go**: per million tokens, $3.00 for the first 100M each month, $2.50 up to 1,000M,
   $2.00 after that; each tier's units at that tier's price. Billed monthly from metering.
3. **Enterprise commit**: 3-year ramp ($60k, $90k, $120k), draws down API usage at $2.00/M, overage at $2.25/M.

Premium Support stays an add-on but goes to $500/month. We want a starter bundle of Team + optional support.

Analytics Classic stops selling at launch; existing customers move to Team when they renew.
Discounts: up to 10% reps can give, 20% Deal Desk, 35% Finance, nothing above that.

Not decided yet: whether Enterprise commit can be paid quarterly, and whether unused commit rolls over.
